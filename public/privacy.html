Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Cyro is a Discord community bot and dashboard operated by Headache Development. This policy explains the information handled by Cyro at cyrobot.ca and through its Discord features.

Server managers choose which bot features to enable. If you participate in a server using Cyro, the enabled features determine which of the information below is processed. A Discord server’s own rules and privacy practices also apply to information shared there.

Information we handle

  • Music and text-to-speech: when a member starts a voice session, Cyro joins that member's voice channel. It does not receive or record microphone audio. Music URLs and playback queues are held temporarily; the selected audio host receives Cyro's server request. For YouTube links, Cyro contacts YouTube for video metadata and audio from Cyro's host; it does not use members' browser cookies or accounts. TTS reads new chat messages from members in the active voice call, processes them locally on Cyro's host, and plays the generated speech into the call. Cyro does not save a speech history or send text to a separate speech service. Server settings and member IDs paired with their selected voice are saved. Queues are cleared on disconnect or restart.
  • Anonymous confessions: public confession embeds do not include the author's name or Discord ID. Discord and Cyro receive the submitting member's identity while processing the slash command; anonymity is to other members, not to Discord. Cyro does not retain the confession text or author ID in its confession records. It retains a counter and up to 1,000 delivery receipts with interaction, channel/message identifiers, timestamps and delivery state to prevent duplicates. Temporary member cooldowns are held in memory and reset on restart. Discord hosts the posted text. Avoid identifying details in the confession itself; server staff can remove posts in Discord.
  • Discord sign-in: your Discord user ID, username, avatar reference, and the server memberships and permissions needed to check access. Cyro receives an OAuth access token from Discord. Your Discord password is entered on Discord, not provided to Cyro.
  • Server configuration: server, channel, role, message, creator, and member identifiers; the settings managers save; announcement templates and filters; and the platform accounts linked to creator profiles.
  • Bot appearance: authorized dashboard users can send a nickname and profile picture for Cyro in their selected server. Uploaded pictures pass through Cyro's host to Discord, which stores and displays the server profile. Cyro does not keep a separate saved copy of the uploaded image. Resetting removes the server override; Discord controls its own media retention.
  • Dashboard access and publishing: owner-configured access roles and command role lists; message/poll publish receipts containing server, channel, message and actor identifiers, timestamps, short titles and content hashes. The bot keeps up to 300 sent and 100 pending receipts per server to identify posts and avoid duplicates. Discord hosts the full published messages, linked media and native poll votes. Cyro does not store voter lists for native Discord polls. Roles granted dashboard access can view and manage server feature records; only the server owner can change access rules.
  • Suggestions and voting: when enabled, Cyro stores suggestion text, the author's Discord ID and display name, channel/message identifiers, timestamps, staff decisions and reasons, and each voter's Discord ID, vote choice and interaction identifier to prevent duplicate votes. Suggestions and vote totals appear in the selected Discord channel and staff dashboard; individual voter IDs and choices are not included in dashboard responses or posted vote totals. Up to 1,000 suggestions per server and 10,000 voter records per suggestion are retained. Older completed suggestions can be removed from bot storage when space is needed; their Discord messages remain. Disabling suggestions stops new submissions and voting but does not delete saved records.
  • Status roles: when a server enables this feature, Cyro compares the custom status Discord makes available for members with the server's configured text, to grant or remove a reward role. Cyro does not save raw member status text or a history of statuses. It stores the configured matching rule, member and role identifiers, and assignment or pending-change receipts needed to manage only roles this feature gave out and recover after restarts. Assignment receipts are removed when their managed role is removed or the member is confirmed to have left. Offline, invisible and privacy-hidden statuses may be unavailable. Managers choose how offline statuses affect existing assignments. Removed-server retention also covers these settings and receipts.
  • Community activity: counting progress, participation scores, invite attribution, levels, and verification outcomes when those features are enabled. Optional invite logging retains up to 1,000 recent join/departure records per server, with member identifiers and names, timestamps, and an inviter, invite code and destination channel when identifiable. These records are available to authorized dashboard users and can also be sent to a server-selected staff channel. Turning logging off stops new records; existing records remain subject to the server retention and deletion process below. Discord copies are not automatically erased. Verification challenges are temporary. Cyro processes messages and relevant Discord events to run enabled features such as counting, spam controls, and word filters.
  • Moderation and support: moderation cases and reasons; ticket participants, staff actions and timestamps; application questions, submitted answers and review decisions. Closing a ticket saves a private transcript on the bot host and in the configured Discord transcript channel before optional channel deletion. Transcripts contain available message and embed text, author identifiers, timestamps and attachment links. Original attachment files are not copied into these transcripts.
  • Optional member image cards: when enabled, Cyro uses the member's Discord display name, avatar and server information to generate a join or leave image on the bot host and post it to the server's selected channel. Avatar images are fetched from Discord; the card renderer does not send this information to a separate image-generation service.
  • Creator alerts and statistics: platform usernames and identifiers, public stream and video metadata, announcement identifiers, delivery status, and measurements available from the provider. Streamer leaderboards measure the streaming time Cyro tracks while its announcements are active; viewer and follower information depends on provider availability.
  • Optional TikTok connection: after you approve TikTok Login Kit, Cyro receives your TikTok account identifier and basic profile information, access and refresh tokens, and metadata for your public videos, including video identifiers, titles, links and publication times. The requested permissions are basic profile access (user.info.basic) and public video access (video.list). Cyro does not request permission to publish to TikTok or read private videos. TikTok LIVE monitoring is a separate, unofficial integration.
  • Technical information: connection information such as IP addresses, request timing, error details and security events may be processed by Cyro and its hosting providers to operate the service and prevent abuse. The dashboard uses essential sign-in and authorization cookies. It does not require advertising cookies.

How we use this information

We use this information to authenticate users, check server and creator permissions, deliver the features selected by server managers, maintain settings across restarts, prevent duplicate alerts, show relevant histories and statistics, and investigate faults or abuse.

When you connect TikTok, Cyro associates the authorized TikTok account with a creator record linked to your Discord member. Cyro uses your basic profile to identify that account and uses your public video list to show recent videos and check for new uploads. A server manager may enable announcements and choose their channel, message, role notification and title filter. Existing uploads are first recorded as a baseline so that enabling alerts does not announce an old backlog.

Who receives information

Discord server members and staff: announcements are visible to people who can see the chosen channel. Moderation logs, tickets, transcripts and applications are available according to that server’s Discord permissions and bot configuration. Server managers and roles granted dashboard access by the server owner can access the settings and feature records needed to administer their server.

Service providers: Cyro currently runs on Prism hosting, and Cloudflare provides the public connection to the dashboard. Discord handles bot interactions and Discord sign-in. TikTok handles TikTok authorization and its APIs. Twitch, Kick and YouTube are contacted when their creator integrations are configured; YouTube is also contacted for requested YouTube music playback. These services process information under their own policies, and processing may occur outside your country.

Access and refresh tokens remain on the server and are not included in normal dashboard responses or Discord announcements. We may also disclose information when legally required or when necessary to address abuse, protect the service, or resolve a request you make.

How long information is kept

Active server settings and feature records are retained while they are needed to run the selected features. Histories are bounded: the bot keeps up to 2,000 moderation cases, 2,000 closed ticket records and 500 completed application records per server, alongside active work. Completed application answers are removed from the bot’s stored application record when the record is processed as completed. Copies already posted to Discord are separate.

Saved ticket transcript files on the bot host are retained separately from the recent ticket list, until the server's bot-managed data is purged or an applicable deletion request is handled. The server-removal retention process includes these files. Copies posted to a Discord transcript channel remain subject to that server's permissions and deletion practices.

Tracked stream statistics use a rolling 90-day history. When Cyro detects that it has been removed from a server, its retention process schedules that server’s bot-managed data for deletion after 30 days. Deletion runs while the bot is operating and connected to Discord; adding Cyro back before deletion cancels that removal schedule.

Discord dashboard sessions are held in memory, expire after no more than eight hours, and become inactive after 30 minutes without authenticated activity. Sign-out removes the session. TikTok authorization is stored separately so approved video checks can continue when your dashboard session ends.

Disconnecting TikTok removes the selected server connection and stops future checks through it. Other server connections you approved for the same TikTok account remain active. When you remove that account’s last connection, Cyro deletes its stored access and refresh tokens and attempts to revoke the authorization with TikTok. Provider access failures may require you to remove Cyro directly in TikTok’s connected-app settings. Previously sent Discord messages, retained server history, operational logs, and backup copies are not automatically erased by disconnecting. Backup and log removal is handled separately when a deletion request is reviewed.

Your choices and requests

Manage or disconnect your TikTok authorization on Creator connections. You can also revoke Cyro in TikTok’s connected-app settings or revoke Discord dashboard access through Discord’s authorized applications. Signing out of the dashboard does not disconnect a creator’s TikTok account.

Server managers can pause alerts, remove creator accounts, change feature settings or remove the bot. To request access, correction or deletion of information about you, contact Headache Development through the Headache Development Discord. Include your Discord user ID and relevant server or creator profile so we can locate the records. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting. Do not post passwords, tokens or private application answers in a public support channel.

For content already posted inside another Discord server, contact that server’s staff as well. Cyro cannot control copies saved by others or records held independently by Discord or another platform. Your rights under applicable privacy law are not limited by this policy.

Age and policy changes

Use of Cyro requires meeting Discord’s minimum age requirements and any additional requirements of a connected platform in your location. Cyro is not directed to children below those requirements. If you believe such information has been supplied, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

We may update this policy when the service or its practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be described through the website or support server as appropriate.

Contact Headache Development
