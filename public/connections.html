What you’re connecting

Cyro asks TikTok for your basic profile and a list of your public videos. You approve this on TikTok’s own sign-in page.

Your profile
Identifies the TikTok account you choose to connect.
Your public videos
Shows recent videos here and lets Cyro announce new uploads in the Discord server you choose.

Server managers choose the announcement channel and optional title filter. Cyro does not upload to TikTok or read private videos.

You can disconnect here at any time. Disconnecting stops future TikTok video checks for that connection; announcements already sent to Discord remain in that server.

TikTok LIVE alerts use a separate, unofficial connection. This sign-in grants access to public uploaded videos, not LIVE analytics.
