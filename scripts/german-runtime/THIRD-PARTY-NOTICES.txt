German voice models are the upstream Piper models converted for sherpa-onnx:
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/tts-models
Each complete MODEL_CARD is retained beside its downloaded model.

de_DE-thorsten-medium: German male, 22,050 Hz.
Dataset and speaker: Thorsten Mueller / Thorsten-Voice; model card declares CC0 dataset.
https://github.com/thorstenMueller/Thorsten-Voice
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/de/de_DE/thorsten/medium

de_DE-kerstin-low: German female, 16,000 Hz.
Dataset: rhasspy/dataset-voice-kerstin; model card declares CC0 dataset.
https://github.com/rhasspy/dataset-voice-kerstin
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/de/de_DE/kerstin/low

The model archive and ONNX file checksums are pinned in models.json. These models
and native binaries are installed on the host, not included in Cyro's source
repository. All inference is local; model downloads do not contain member speech.
